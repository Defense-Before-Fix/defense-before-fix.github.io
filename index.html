Defense Before Fix

A phase that runs before a defect is fixed: the instance is treated as evidence of a class, and the defence that detects the class is built and seen to fire before the fix is made. It is published under the British spelling; this site exists so that the US spelling finds the right place.

The home page: the definition, the six clauses, the documents, the tools that implement the method and the article in which it was first published.
